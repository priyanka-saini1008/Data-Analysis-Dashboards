# Data Analytics Dashboards

Three dashboards built with Power BI and Advanced Excel to turn raw data into clear insights.

## 1. Apparel Sales & Customer Insights Dashboard (Excel)
An Excel dashboard that shows clothing sales and customer behavior in one view.

- Data: 29 sample orders
- KPIs for orders, quantity, delivery days, amount and customer rating
- Sales by product and by order channel
- Built with Pivot Tables, charts and slicers
- **Insight:** Jeans was the top-selling product, and the App was the best order channel


## 2. Blinkit Grocery Sales Dashboard (Power BI)
An interactive dashboard that tracks sales, ratings and item visibility across Blinkit outlets.

- Data: 8,500+ grocery records
- Cleaned the Item Fat Content column (LF and low fat changed to Low Fat, reg changed to Regular)
- KPI cards, donut chart, funnel chart, stacked area chart, bar chart and pivot table
- Slicers for Item Type, Outlet Size and Outlet Location Type
- **Insight:** Tier 3 outlets had the highest sales, and Fruits & Vegetables was the top-selling category

## 3. Employee Salary Dashboard (Excel)
An Excel dashboard that compares employee salary across different groups.

- Data: 50 employee records
- Salary analysis by gender, education level, city, department and job role
- KPI cards for total salary, average age, total of rept departments and total of rept cities
- Built with Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts and slicers
- **Insight:** Marketing had the highest total salary, and Delhi had the highest salary by city
